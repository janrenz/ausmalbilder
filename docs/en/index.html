How to print the colouring pages

  1. Click a picture.
  2. Click “Download PDF” or simply choose “Print”.
  3. In the print dialog, set the paper size to A4 and choose “Fit to page”. Greyscale is perfectly fine.

All pictures are original designs, free of well-known characters and brands. You can use and copy them for free at home, in nursery, at school and in clubs. More about usage.

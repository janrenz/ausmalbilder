When you visit the site, the host processes data transmitted by your browser in so-called log files: IP address, date and time of the request, requested address, referrer, HTTP status code, amount of data transferred, and information about your browser and operating system.

Visitor statistics with Umami

To find out which colouring pages are popular, I count page views and clicks on “Download PDF”, “Print” and “PNG” using the open-source software Umami. Umami runs on my own server (umami-wwuskowincn5xs0nx5pucyc0.uds.university), sets no cookies, stores nothing on your device and does not build profiles across websites.

The following is recorded: the page visited, the referring page, browser, operating system, device type, screen size, language, and the country, region and city derived from the IP address. The IP address itself is not stored; returning visits are only recognised through a monthly rotating, irreversible hash. If your browser sends a “Do Not Track” signal, nothing is recorded.

The legal basis is my legitimate interest in privacy-friendly audience measurement (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). The statistics server is connected via Cloudflare (Cloudflare Inc., 101 Townsend St, San Francisco, CA 94107, USA), which processes the connection data as a processor and is certified under the EU-US Data Privacy Framework.

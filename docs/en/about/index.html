About the Pictures and How to Use Them

May I use the pictures?

Yes. All colouring pages on this site are in the public domain and available under CC0 1.0. You can print them, copy them, hand them out at nursery, at school or in a club, modify them and share them without asking, and without crediting the source. I would still be happy about a link.

How were the pictures made?

The designs were created with an AI image model (Google Gemini) from my own descriptions, then checked by hand, sorted and prepared for printing. Pictures containing text, logos or any resemblance to well-known characters were discarded and made again.

There are deliberately no characters from films, series or books (such as from Disney, Paw Patrol or Pokémon) and no real car brands: these are protected by copyright and trade mark law and must not be freely distributed as colouring pages. Instead you will find original characters and entirely made-up vehicles.

What age are they for?

Most topics are drawn for children aged 3 to 8: thick lines and big areas. Fashion, cars, space and technology are more detailed and intended for children from about 8. The mandalas for adults are very fine and ideal for relaxing.

Spotted a mistake or want a new design?

If you notice a picture that does not fit, or you would like a particular topic, write to kontakt@janrenz.de.
